Privacy

Small shop site, small print.

This website sets no cookies and carries no third-party trackers or scripts — there is nothing running in the background while you read.

The only third-party content is the Google Maps embed on the visit page. When a map loads, Google receives standard request data (such as an IP address) the same way it does for any embedded map.

The contact form collects what you type into it — your name, phone, email, and message — so the note can be read and answered. It is not sold, and it is not used for anything else.

The full record catalog lives on the shop’s own store site, thebeatdistrict.co, which keeps its own cookie and privacy practices.

Questions about anything on this page: info@thebeatdistrict.co.
