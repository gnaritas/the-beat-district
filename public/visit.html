Good to know

Before you dig

Is online stock the same as in-store stock?

No — the shop’s own note is plain: “In Stock” online means quick order fulfillment, generally 2–3 business days, and online prices can differ from the shop floor.

Can I pick up online orders in Petersburg?

Yes — free local pickup in Petersburg is offered on online orders.

Do you stock local artists?

Yes — records from local artists are part of the shop’s pick list.

Who’s behind the counter

A new shop, run by the person picking the records

The Beat District opened in fall 2026 and is run by Demetrius Frazier, who also runs Resist Booksellers just down the street. Coverage of the opening notes that he hand-picks the vinyl himself — from fresh indie releases to best sellers, audiophile-grade pressings, local artist records, and vintage staples.

Source: 12onyourside.com, “What’s New in RVA”, November 2, 2026.
