How pre-orders work

Release date, label, pressing color

Upcoming releases open for pre-order on the shop site with the release date, the label, and the pressing called out — indie-exclusive orange, midnight purple, stoney orange, neon pink. Anniversary editions and Record Store Day releases ride the same list.

Quick order fulfillment, generally 2–3 business days. The shop’s own note: “In Stock” online means quick order fulfillment — online prices and stock can differ from the shop floor.

Free local pickup in Petersburg. Order online, pick it up in Petersburg.
